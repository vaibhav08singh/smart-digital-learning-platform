# CodeZen — Core Feature Matrix

CodeZen implements 6 comprehensive pillars designed for student empowerment across all educational levels:

---

### ☀️ 1. 3D Course Solar System
- **Visual Metaphor**: The central glowing Sun represents **CODEZEN**, while orbiting CGI planets represent active courses (*Data Structures*, *Full-Stack Web Dev*, *AI & Neural Networks*, *Cloud & DevOps*, *Cybersecurity*, *Physics & Light*, *Quadratic Equations*, *English Grammar*).
- **Interactive Controls**: Pure 3D circular concentric orbits, left-right camera tilt control, and cursor-hover course name tooltips.

---

### 🤖 2. Intelligent AI Tutor & CS Knowledge Engine
- **RAG Architecture**: Powered by a 130KB+ structured computer science knowledge base (`src/data/cs-knowledge.ts`).
- **Comprehensive Responses**: Answers questions with step-by-step breakdowns, code examples, interview preparation questions, exam tips, and real-world analogies.

---

### 💻 3. Multi-Language Interactive Coding Lab
- **Piston Integration**: Compiles and executes code instantly across C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Go, Rust, and SQL.
- **AI Debugging & Assistance**: Integrated AI assistant offers code refactoring, bug explanations, and performance optimizations.

---

### 🎓 4. Adaptive Grade & Degree Progression Engine
- **18 Educational Tiers**: Seamlessly adapts user dashboard, subjects, and difficulty level from Class 1 to MTech/Research.
- **Custom Learning Paths**: Dynamic subject selection tailored to school or university grade level.

---

### 📊 5. Mastery Analytics & Weakness Tracking
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Tracks concept mastery percentages, time spent per subject, and quiz performance.
- **Weakness Identification**: Pins specific weak topics and suggests targeted practice modules.

---

### 📚 6. Video & Resource Hub
- **Curated Video Lessons**: Integrates YouTube educational videos with topic timestamps.
- **Study Resources**: PDF notes, cheat sheets, and downloadable formula sheets.
